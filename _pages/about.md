I am **Xuanchang Zhang** (章轩畅), a first-year PhD student at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu), advised by Prof. [Tong Zhang](https://tongzhang-ml.org).

My research focuses on **post-training and alignment of large language models**, with an emphasis on reinforcement learning. My recent work focuses on training LLMs with reinforcement learning using off-policy data.

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn), where I was a member of the [ACM Honors Class](https://acm.sjtu.edu.cn/home). During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Prof. [Zhuosheng Zhang](https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/~zhangzs/) and Prof. [Hai Zhao](https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/~zhaohai/) at SJTU, and later with Prof. Tong Zhang at UIUC.
